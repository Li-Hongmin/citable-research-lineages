# Retained challenges and correction scope

- Short-sum S must include ordinary mu AND Lambda−1 over the specified starts and prefixes; bibliography alone is not inspection of the original theorem.
- Fixed smooth weights, M=2Y, H and L ranges, nonempty window and fixed/growing gcd constants are carried.
- Paid Fejer localization bounds a root-energy difference, not an unconditional energy-difference saving.
- The complete off-diagonal signed arithmetic Gram target, all cofactors and both parity channels remain unproved.
- Round5 preserves the scope of the earlier non-arithmetic countermodel; Lambda support and Lambda=mu*log exclude it as genuine arithmetic.
- Finite diagnostics and archival run outputs do not certify asymptotics or RH.

VERSIONS.json records source-level challenge/correction annotations without creating fictitious CRL IDs. All historical assertions and countermodels remain unreviewed. Unsaved prepatch bytes are not reconstructed or claimed as exact prior versions.
