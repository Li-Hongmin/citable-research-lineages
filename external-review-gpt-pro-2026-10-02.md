# Design-review concerns

This public-facing summary retains the issues to test without reproducing the private review conversation or its account metadata.

- Compare against Git and nanopublications with the same structured research objects and budget; existing provenance and publication mechanisms limit novelty claims.
- Define commitments before calling different views or omissions equivocation. Retrieval failure alone is not cryptographic evidence of malicious withholding.
- Preserve later incoming challenges and failed replications when exporting an earlier claim; document snapshot-relative coverage and missing records.
- Treat identity, resource limits, discovery under spam, and human attribution as separate unresolved questions.
- Evaluate interoperable dispute preservation before claiming a scientific-productivity benefit or a need for new network infrastructure.

These are design questions, not an independent verification of the prototype or any mathematical contribution.
